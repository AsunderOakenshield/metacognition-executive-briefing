How do we evaluate global impact without ignoring local differences?

Why this matters to Google

Global initiatives in employee development, learning, and AI adoption must balance consistent evaluation with diverse local cultures, resources, and organizational needs.

Project background

A longitudinal evaluation of a global learning initiative, examining implementation and outcomes across diverse school contexts.

0102 The Approach

How we measured implementation and impact

  1. Triangulate multiple signals

    Surveys, interviews, observations, outcome measures, and platform records captured different views of the program.

  2. Monitor continuously

    Dashboards and tailored reporting supported ongoing monitoring, timely feedback, and adjustment.

  3. Preserve local flexibility

    Schools and teachers adapted implementation to local needs and tools, while the evaluation maintained shared measures for comparison.

Takeaway

Global programs can be evaluated consistently without being implemented identically.

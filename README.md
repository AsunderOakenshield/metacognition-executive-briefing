# Metacognition Executive Briefing

[Open the two-slide briefing](https://asunderoakenshield.github.io/metacognition-executive-briefing/)

This presentation is an example of communicating research findings to a non-academic audience. It turns a multi-year evaluation of a global metacognition initiative into a short briefing for leaders and teams who need to understand the findings and decide what to do with them.

## What the example shows

The first slide leads with the practical takeaway. The second uses a heatmap, teacher-reported findings, and interview examples to show how implementation differed across schools and what teachers observed. The format gives readers enough evidence to understand the takeaway at a glance.

## Why it matters to global teams

Teams at Google and other global organizations face a similar evaluation question when they roll out learning or adoption initiatives across regions. Common measures can surface differences in use or outcomes. Local information about resources, connectivity, cultural context, and organizational support helps teams interpret those differences and decide where to investigate or provide support.

The briefing is available in six languages and includes a color-vision-friendly palette and a print layout.

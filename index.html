01 The Takeaway02

Scaling globally doesn’t
mean implementing identically.

For Google’s global initiatives, meaningful comparisons require standardized measurement, not identical implementation. Evaluating performance and effectiveness also requires understanding local resources, cultural context, and organizational support.

A global metacognition education initiative spanning 30+ schools, 20+ countries, and 3+ years.

0102 The Evidence

Different implementation paths
can still lead to positive outcomes.

  • 69% of teachers reported technological barriers.

    Interviews showed that teachers used alternative platforms and paper-based activities to continue teaching metacognition.

  • 61% of teachers reported improved student metacognition.

    Teachers perceived gains in student learning even as implementation varied across schools.

Sources: Honoring Individuality through the Evaluation of a Global Metacognition Project (AERA 2027 accepted proposal) · Nord Anglia Education, Metacognition Project Impact Report (2025) · Project interviews and observations
